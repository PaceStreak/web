How it works

Three taps. That's the whole ritual.

  1. 01

    Log the session

    Lift, run, ride, climb. Pick the type, tap done. Detail is optional — the point is that it took ten seconds, so you'll actually do it on the bad days.

  2. 02

    Keep the streak alive

    Set your own week — three days, five, six. PaceStreak counts against your target, so a planned rest day never costs you the run you've built.

  3. 03

    Watch the grid fill

    Six months of squares is a harder thing to abandon than a to-do list. The grid is the receipt, and it's the reason you go on a Tuesday in February.

Features

Built for people who show up.

🎯 Streaks that respect rest

Rest is training. Define your weekly target and recovery days count as kept, not broken. No guilt-tripping over a deload week.

📈 Progress that compounds

Volume, frequency and PRs over months — not a single session's numbers. See the trend line that actually predicts results.

⚡ Ten-second logging

The fastest log wins. Anything that takes longer gets skipped when you're tired, and skipped logs kill streaks.

🏋️ Any discipline

Barbell, trail, tarmac or wall. One streak across everything, or separate chains per discipline — your call.

🔒 Your data, exportable

Full JSON and CSV export from day one. It's your training history; you should be able to walk out with it.

📱 Works offline

Gym basements have terrible signal. Log offline, it syncs when you're back above ground.

FAQ

Reasonable questions.

Is a streak just a guilt machine?

It is, if it's built badly. That's why the streak counts against a target you set, rest days count as kept, and you can repair a missed day once a month. The goal is showing up over a year, not a perfect record you abandon in week three.

Do I have to log sets, reps and weight?

No. A session counts with one tap. Detail is there when you want it — for PRs and volume trends — but it is never required to keep the chain.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta. The plan afterwards is a free tier that keeps working and a paid tier for deeper history and analysis. Your data stays exportable either way.

When can I use it?

It's early. This page is the honest state of things: the product is being built in the open, and early access goes out in batches rather than all at once. Mail hello@pacestreak.com and you'll be in the next one.
